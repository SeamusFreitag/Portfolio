About

My career started in logistics and analytics, where I learned that every problem leaves clues. Whether it was tracking down operational bottlenecks, analyzing performance data, or finding the cause of a breakdown in a process, I enjoyed digging into systems and figuring out how they worked.

That same curiosity is what pulled me into cybersecurity.

Today, I am focused on building a career in Blue Team and Purple Team operations. I enjoy understanding how systems fail, how attackers think, and how organizations can better defend themselves. Through hands-on labs, home lab projects, and continuous learning, I am developing practical skills in threat detection, security operations, system administration, and network security.

I believe good security comes from equal parts technical knowledge, analytical thinking, and relentless curiosity. The technology will always change, but the mindset of learning, adapting, and solving problems remains the same.

When I'm not studying cybersecurity, you'll usually find me building projects, experimenting in my home lab, or finding new ways to break things so I can learn how to secure them.
